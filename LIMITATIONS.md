# Limitations

The restoration outputs were produced by the actual release-60 RasterPageProcessor integrated path on a CPU-only iOS Simulator. Native Compare, Review, Sign and Redaction outputs/captures used the frozen release-60 simulator app. This establishes example provenance; it is not a physical-device speed benchmark, legal certification or universal result guarantee.

No externally retouched restoration output is included. PDF display PNGs were rendered from actual exported PDFs. Important OCR text must be checked against the source. Device, OS, language and input affect OCR and Intelligence. The current iPhone marketing Intelligence example is rejected because no supported key facts were shown; it is not included here as positive proof.

Optional iCloud and Apple system processing paths may vary. Two signed-in physical-device iCloud qualification is not claimed. Screen protection cannot universally prevent screenshots or recording. PDF import examples are single-file imports, not batch-PDF evidence.

The supplied redacted copy passed the recorded date-specific visual, PDF text and raster OCR checks, with a positive OCR control on the original. This is a bounded example, not a proof for every file or recovery method. Inspect your completed exported copy, and retain originals separately as sensitive material.

PDF compatibility observation: the supplied actual Service_Agreement_Reviewed.pdf renders in Poppler, but Poppler reports an incorrect stream length / missing endstream diagnostic. The exported bytes are preserved unchanged rather than repaired for this demonstration. The other five reviewer PDFs rendered without diagnostics. Check compatibility in your own viewer; no claim of universally flawless PDF export is made.
