# DocMate 2.0 — real-work stories

DocMate is being tested around **real document workflows**, not generic feature checklists. The stories below use genuine DocMate 2.0 screenshots. They describe handoffs and app-switching that the workflow is designed to remove; they are not processing-speed benchmarks.

## Legal & contracts

### “They say they only changed one date. Do you trust that sentence?”

A lawyer or contract administrator gets a revised service agreement by email. The sender says the only change is the renewal date.

**Before:** open two PDFs, zoom back and forth, remember which page changed, then move to another app to sign.

**With DocMate:** Compare the two PDFs → Review the actual change → Sign → Save and reopen the final file.

  <img src="https://docmate.me/assets/apple/docmate-2/v2/crops/compare-ipad-detail.png" alt="DocMate 2.0 Compare on iPad" width="60%">

**What it removes:** manual page hunting, version anxiety, a separate signing handoff.

**Legal / contracts TestFlight:** https://testflight.apple.com/join/e5dv3qug

## Sales & remote client work

### “Your client is in another city. The contract still needs signing tonight.”

A sales rep or account manager sends a proposal or contract through Mail, WhatsApp or WeChat. The customer is not going to meet in person.

**Before:** send PDF → explain where to sign → wait for a scan/photo or another signing tool → download the returned file → check it again.

**With DocMate:** Review → Sign → Save/share back from the phone.

## What we want from testers

We are not trying to maximize raw TestFlight installs. We want to know:

1. Did this story match something you actually do?
2. Which step did DocMate remove — and which step is still annoying?
3. Would you keep DocMate installed for the next time this happens?
4. Would you tell a colleague, classmate, client-facing teammate, or friend about it?

Specific criticism is more useful than praise. Use fictional or privacy-safe documents only.
