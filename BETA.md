## Who will give the most useful feedback?

Please join if you use PDFs, scans, forms, receipts, contracts, study material, or document signing on iPhone/iPad at least weekly and can complete at least two missions below.

We value specific criticism more than praise. Tell us what is slow, confusing, missing, or unexpectedly good.

## Five testing missions

### 1. Review → Sign → Save → Reopen
Import a multi-page PDF. Enter Review, add signatures or marks to more than one page, save, close, and reopen. Check that placements remain correct and that export matches preview.

### 2. Scan → Improve → Save
Scan at least three pages. Try crop, rotation and document filters, including one landscape page. Check page order, thumbnails, preview, save, reopen and export.

### 3. Compare → Review
Compare two similar PDFs with a meaningful change such as a date, amount or clause. Check whether differences are understandable and whether moving from Compare to Review feels obvious.

### 4. iCloud on two devices
If you have two devices using the same Apple Account, create or import a document on one and check document/folder/signature sync on the other. Record what synced and roughly how long it took.

### 5. Stress it
Try a long or image-heavy PDF, Search, Workspace or Activity. Report slow loading, repeated refreshing, missing thumbnails, heat, memory pressure, crashes, or anything that makes the app feel unreliable.

## Send feedback

The fastest method is **TestFlight screenshot feedback**: take a screenshot while using DocMate, annotate it if useful, then submit it through TestFlight.

For a structured bug report, use the GitHub template:

**[Submit DocMate beta feedback](https://github.com/ewardlee/docmate-public-tests/issues/new?template=beta-feedback.yml)**

If you do not use GitHub — or if you want to opt in to future beta follow-up — use the short DocMate feedback form:

**[Share real-world beta feedback / join the Founding Tester Circle](https://forms.gle/5Zo7hoFX7rJVKH5x7)**

The form does not require a Google sign-in and does not ask you to upload documents. Contact details are optional.

Useful reports include:
- build number, device and OS version;
- exact steps;
- expected result;
- actual result;
- whether it happens every time;
- a privacy-safe screenshot or recording when useful.

**Do not upload private documents, signatures, IDs, financial statements or confidential business files.** Use the fictional samples in this repository or redact sensitive information.

## Founding Beta Circle

People who complete several missions or submit especially actionable reports may be invited to future betas and, with permission, acknowledged as early contributors.

This is recognition for honest testing. It is **not payment or a request for a positive App Store review or rating**.
