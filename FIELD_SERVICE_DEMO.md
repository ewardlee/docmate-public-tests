# A work order you can test in minutes

**Picture this:** You're finishing an HVAC job. The customer is at the door and wants a signed copy of the handover paperwork before you drive to the next site. You have only your iPhone, and reception may be poor.

No real customer document needed. This fictional PDF has the full work order on page 1 and a blank customer-signature area on page 2.

## The files

**[Open / download ready-made work order V1 (PDF)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewardlee/docmate-public-tests/main/Inputs/Field_Service_Work_Order_V1.pdf)**

**[Open / download revised work order V2 (PDF)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ewardlee/docmate-public-tests/main/Inputs/Field_Service_Work_Order_V2.pdf)**

- Work order: FS-DEMO-2046. All names, addresses, work and prices are invented.
- V1: 4 filters, completed at 16:20, USD 285, no follow-up.
- V2: 5 filters, completed at 17:05, USD 325, follow-up within 14 days.
- Both files are two-page **synthetic input documents**, not output generated by DocMate. They are independent beta fixtures, not part of the release-60 output provenance manifest.
- No paid plan, trial, verification guarantee or feature entitlement is promised by these samples.

## Mission A — at the customer's door

1. **[Install the field-service TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/EPaX6wbH)** on iPhone or iPad.
2. Download **V1** above first. Open/import it in DocMate through Files or the share sheet.
3. Turn on **Airplane Mode** to simulate a weak/no-signal site. In DocMate, open **Review**, inspect pages 1 and 2, and try adding a fictional name, mark or signature in the empty signature area on page 2. No actual customer signature.
4. Save the reviewed copy. Close the document and reopen it. **Did the signature stay in the right place? Did the file remain accessible offline?** Note any errors or unexpected permission/subscription prompts.
5. Turn Airplane Mode off. Export/share the finished copy to Files or another app. Inspect that exported PDF outside DocMate. Do not assume offline syncing or delivery happened while disconnected.

This is a **test**, not a claim that every step has been confirmed to work in all conditions. If a step fails, that's useful feedback.

## Mission B — the office sends a revised work order

1. Download **V2** above.
2. Import V1 and V2 as separate files.
3. In **Compare**, compare V1 against V2.
4. Can you quickly identify the change in completion time (16:20 to 17:05), quantity (4 to 5 filters), total (USD 285 to 325) and follow-up instructions?
5. Tell us if Compare makes the revision clearer than manually checking both pages, and note any locked or inaccessible feature.
